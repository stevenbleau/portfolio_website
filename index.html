.02 · About

About me

  • Steven Bleau · Full-stack product design

    I take digital and physical products from research to launch, and I can build the parts I design. I founded Telo Design and led product development there, shipping my own products end to end.

    I've worked with Fortune 500 companies, consultancies, startups, and non-profits, across industries from transportation to medtech. I got to full stack the long way: a B.S. in Product Design from the University of Minnesota formalized the physical craft, a full-stack web development certification took me front end to back end, and an M.S. in Robotics in mechatronics, controls, and machine learning is where hardware and software meet. Design school, then code, then robotics.

About

Steven Bleau

Product designer and engineer. I take digital and physical products from research to launch — and I can build the parts I design.

Where I've worked

I've worked with Fortune 500 companies, small consultancies, startups, non-profits, social enterprises, and education organizations — across industries from transportation to medtech.

The throughline is ownership: leading projects where research, industrial design, mechanical work, software, and production all have to line up. My background spans industrial and UX design, web development, robotics, machine learning, and mechanical design.

How I got here

After a short stint in engineering, I transferred into the product design program at the University of Minnesota, where I finished a B.S. in Product Design.

After graduating, I founded Telo Design and led product development — designing, engineering, and shipping my own products. That work pushed me deeper into the technical stack: a full-stack development certification, then an M.S. in Robotics.

The result is a designer who is effective across domains and stages — equally at home with a client interview, a CAD model, a circuit, or a codebase — and who has run the whole process, end-to-end, as an owner.
